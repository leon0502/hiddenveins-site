关于游戏About the Game01 · ABOUT

《隐脉勘舆》是一款以真实测绘为核心的开放世界 RPG。踏勘、测量、绘图——没有一键扫图，每一张地图都由你亲手测出，并带你走向这片大陆的更深处。

Hidden Veins is an open-world RPG about the real craft of surveying. Scout, measure, draw — there is no one-click map reveal. Every map is one you surveyed yourself, and each one leads you deeper into the land.

游戏取材于开发者本人的测绘工作经历：野外的蚊虫、泥泞与天气，出毛病的仪器，以及天黑前收工、在灯下把一天的数据绘成地图的那份踏实。

It draws on the developer's own experience as a field surveyor: mosquitoes, mud and weather, instruments that act up, and the quiet satisfaction of packing up before dark and turning a day's data into a map by lamplight.

核心玩法Core Gameplay02 · GAMEPLAY

亲手测量Measure It Yourself

架设三脚架、调平气泡、对中测站，通过目镜照准远处的立标。距离、雨雾、密林遮挡都会影响读数——精度靠的是你的手感。

Plant the tripod, center the bubble, sight a distant target through the eyepiece. Distance, rain, fog and dense forest all affect your readings — accuracy comes from your hands.

把数据绘成地图Turn Data into Maps

回到营地整理手簿、检查闭合误差，连点成线、勾出等高线与地物。测得越准，地图越可靠，也越能为你指出新的道路。

Back at camp, check your field book and closure error, connect the points and draw contours and features. The more precise your survey, the more your map can be trusted — and the more paths it opens.

扛住野外Survive the Field

站得越久，蚊群越多；泥地让脚架下陷，天气打乱计划，仪器还会莫名出毛病。排查故障、在天黑前带着当天的数据回营地。

The longer you stand still, the bigger the mosquito swarm. Mud sinks your tripod, weather wrecks your plans, and gear fails for no clear reason. Troubleshoot, and make it back to camp before dark.
