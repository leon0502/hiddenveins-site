Over these few days, Hidden Veins got a Chapter 1 blueprint we can build from, and delivered its first piece of surveying you can actually play. Surveyor Lin Che heads into a valley that a reservoir is about to flood, and uses real surveying methods to re-survey an incomplete ancient map.

这几天，《隐脉勘舆》（Hidden Veins）有了可以按图施工的第一章蓝图，也交出了第一段真正能玩的测量操作。测量员林澈将走进即将被水库淹没的山谷，用真实的测绘方法，把一张残缺的古舆图重新测出来。

November 16: A fresh start11 月 16 日：项目重新出发

Work was brought together into the current main project, which keeps the name 《隐脉勘舆》 / Hidden Veins. The same day we built playtest v0.4: you start with no equipment and first have to fetch the tripod and instrument from camp, then set up, record, drive and save the way real fieldwork is done. The valley is about 300 × 300 metres, and this build is still a vertical slice of roughly ten-odd minutes, meant to nail down how fieldwork feels.

工作合并到现在的主项目，游戏名沿用《隐脉勘舆》/ Hidden Veins。同一天打出了试玩版 v0.4：开局身上没有器材，要先去营地取三脚架和仪器，再按外业习惯架站、记录、开车和存档。山谷约 300 × 300 米，这一版仍是大约十几分钟的垂直切片，用来立住外业手感。

The story direction is set too. The main storyline is “The Survey Scroll”: while doing control surveying before the reservoir fills, Lin Che finds an incomplete map left behind long ago, and starts re-surveying it, one clue at a time. His mentor’s storyline stays as an optional side story. The puzzles use old Chinese units of length, which you convert into metres as you go. The interface is English-first with Chinese second, and both languages follow the same main story.

故事方向也定了。主线是「舆图残卷」：蓄水前的控制测量里，林澈发现一张古人留下的残缺舆图，于是顺着图上的线索一点点重测。师父线作为可选支线保留。谜题里用的是古代的长度单位，要边算边换成米。界面以英文为主、中文为辅，中英文走同一条主线。

November 17: Pinning down Chapter 1’s skeleton11 月 17 日：把第一章的骨架钉住

That day we settled 46 open questions from sections 18.10–18.16 of the design document, and then gave the mentor a name: Gao Yuan. Chapter 1 is set in Heng Ridge, a fictional karst mountain region in the southwest, which uses its own Heng Ridge Local Grid. Level layouts were written into the document. Years ago, Gao Yuan surveyed these mountains, but part of the way was left unfinished — that part, Lin Che has to survey himself.

这一天拍板了设计文档第 18.10–18.16 节共 46 个问题，随后师父定名为高远（Gao Yuan）。第一章的舞台是虚构的西南喀斯特山区衡岭（Heng Ridge），采用衡岭独立坐标系。关卡落位写进了文档。高远多年前在这片山里做过普查，却有一段路没有走完——那一段，要由林澈自己测完。

November 18–19: Chapter 1 puzzle sites locked in11 月 18–19 日：第一章谜题遗迹定稿

Over the next two days, the puzzle sites in the second half of Chapter 1 were settled one by one, and each is built around a real surveying method. To keep things spoiler-free, here we only say which kind of method each one uses:

接下来两天，第一章后半的谜题遗迹一处一处定下来，每处都对应一种真实的测量方法。为了不剧透，这里只说每处用的是哪类方法：

  • The North Ridge Mark — long-distance sighting. Get up high, look out along a single line, and search the ridge for what is waiting there.

    北脊刻字：远距离照准。登上高处，沿一条视线望出去，在山脊上寻找等着你的东西。

  • The Nested Halls — carrying a bearing inside and running a closed traverse. In a place built to get you lost, the only thing you can trust is the angles you measure yourself, station by station.

    回字廊：方位传递与闭合导线。在一个专门让人迷路的地方，唯一能信的，是你自己一站一站测出来的角度。

  • The Well Archive — carrying heights and directions down a shaft, then setting out below. You take what you know on the surface down into a place you can’t see.

    天井藏经洞：竖井传递高程和方向，再在井下放样。把地面上已知的东西，带进看不见的深处。

  • The Thousand Rooms — resection. In rooms that all look alike, work out where you are standing from angles alone, and learn to judge whether your answer can be trusted.

    千室厅：后方交会。在长得一模一样的房间里，只凭测角弄清自己站在哪里，还要学会判断这个结果靠不靠得住。

  • The Hidden Channel — levelling and chainage. Down in the dark, a level and a measured distance are what keep you on course.

    暗渠：水准测量与量里程。在黑暗里，靠水准仪和量出来的距离认路。

  • The Confluence Hall — a breakthrough survey. Measure above ground and below, and make the two halves meet.

    汇水殿：贯通测量。地上、地下各测一段，让两边的成果严丝合缝地对上。

  • And beyond — the end of Chapter 1 leads straight into Chapter 2.

    再往后：第一章的终点，直接通向第二章。

On the evening of November 19, the Chapter 1 design was officially frozen. Any changes from here on will go into the change log. Chapter 2 will be written once Chapter 1 has been built in the game.

11 月 19 日傍晚，第一章设计正式冻结。之后若再改，会记进变更记录。第二章等第一章在游戏里做出来再写。

Over the same period, we started preparing the ground surface for Heng Ridge. After Quixel made the entire Megascans library free on Fab under the Standard License, we drew up a shortlist of candidates for Chapter 1 and test-imported three assets: mossy rocky ground, mossy forest stones and grass clumps. They went into a Godot 4.7.2 greybox test scene, and we built a Windows preview, HiddenVeins_MegascansPreview. Close-up detail already shows; at a distance the tiling still repeats, the grass blades are a little dark, and the frame rate still has to be measured on the target PC.

同一时期，我们开始给衡岭准备地表。Quixel 把整套 Megascans 在 Fab 上按标准许可免费开放后，先为第一章列了候选清单，并试着导入了三件：苔藓岩地、苔藓林石和草丛。它们进了 Godot 4.7.2 的灰盒测试场景，也打出了 Windows 预览版 HiddenVeins_MegascansPreview。近处细节已经能看出来；远处平铺还有重复，草叶偏暗，帧率也还要在目标电脑上实测。

November 19, evening: Station setup is playable11 月 19 日晚：架站可以玩了

The same night, the first step of milestone M1 became playable: setting up the station, centring, levelling and measuring the instrument height. When you pick a spot, the ground shows colour bands by slope; above about 15° you can’t set up. Once the tripod legs are spread, you use the optical plummet (C) to centre the instrument over the point mark. After fine levelling you have to check again, because fine levelling often nudges the instrument slightly off centre. Keep the bubble centred for a moment and it locks, or press Space to confirm. Hold H to measure the instrument height; if you forget, the record notes that it was never measured. On soft ground the tripod slowly sinks, up to 5 millimetres per station, and the bubble drifts with it. If the remaining tilt is beyond the compensator’s range, the instrument shows TILT. All of these rules have been covered by automated tests against the acceptance table in section 18.3.1 of the design document. Every test passed, and we built a Windows package, HiddenVeins_M1Step1. Still to come in M1: survey records, closure and blunders, saving, rod partner Zhou Ning, the map, and commissions.

同一晚，里程碑 M1 的第一步做到了可玩：架站、对中、调平、量仪器高。选点时地面会按坡度给出色带，超过约 15° 就不能架。展开脚架后，要用对中器（C）把仪器中心对到点位标志上；精平之后还要复查，因为精平常会让仪器中心微微挪开。气泡居中并保持片刻即可锁定，也可以按空格确认。按住 H 量仪器高；若忘了量，记录里会记下没有量过。软土上脚架会缓慢下沉，单站最多 5 毫米，气泡也会跟着走。残余倾斜超出补偿器范围时，仪器会提示 TILT。上述规则已按设计文档第 18.3.1 节验收表做了自动测试，全部通过，并打出了 Windows 包 HiddenVeins_M1Step1。M1 后面还有测量记录、闭合与粗差、存档、跑尺搭档周宁、地图和委托。

Art also delivered its first round of planning and built a base mesh for Zhou Ning, about 1.76 metres tall, which is already imported into Godot. Zhou Ning is a technician on loan from the Water Bureau; his main job is holding the prism pole. Lin Che’s and Zhou Ning’s heads have also been iterated to a new version.

美术也交了第一轮规划，并做出了周宁约 1.76 米的底模，已导入 Godot。周宁是水利站借来的技术员，主要工作是立棱镜杆。林澈和周宁的头部也迭代到了新一版。

November 20: Same survey area, comparing engines11 月 20 日：同一测区，比较引擎

Using the same survey area from v0.4 and the same Megascans assets, we made same-camera comparisons in Godot, Unity and Unreal. Right now we are leaning toward giving Unity a try, but we have not officially switched engines. Until we have played it ourselves, the M1 plan on Godot stays as it is. Tonight we start moving the most essential piece of feel — Lin Che walking in third person, setting up a level once, and reading the rod once — into Unity, and building a playtest package.

我们用 v0.4 的同一块测区、同一套 Megascans 素材，在 Godot、Unity 和 Unreal 里做了同机位对比。目前更倾向于试一试 Unity，但还没有正式换引擎。在亲手试玩之前，Godot 上的 M1 安排不变。今晚开始把最核心的一段手感——林澈第三人称走路、架一次水准仪、读一次尺——迁到 Unity 里，打一个试玩包。

What’s next下一步

Keep finishing M1’s survey records, closure and blunders, and saving, and bring Zhou Ning into the scene; apply the imported scanned materials to the real valley floor; and once the Unity playtest build is out, play through it ourselves before deciding on the engine. Chapter 2’s design and new terrain are on hold until Chapter 1 runs in the game.

继续完成 M1 的测量记录、闭合与粗差、存档，并让周宁走进场景；把已导入的扫描材质用到真正的河谷地面上；等 Unity 试玩包出来，亲手走一遍后再决定引擎。第二章的设计和新的地形先放下，等第一章在游戏里跑通。

The water is still chasing this valley. For now, we keep the tripod steady.

这条河谷还在被水追着。我们先把脚架支稳。

Update (Nov 21)更新（11 月 21 日）

After reviewing the results of the Unity test migration, we officially decided on November 21 to switch to Unity and have started planning the migration. The render pipeline (URP or HDRP) will be chosen after a side-by-side comparison. The line above saying we had “not officially switched engines” is now out of date.

看完 Unity 试迁移的结果后，我们在 11 月 21 日正式决定换用 Unity，并开始计划迁移。渲染管线（URP 或 HDRP）等同机位对比后再定。上文 11 月 20 日一节「还没有正式换引擎」的说法已经过时。
